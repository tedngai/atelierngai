Ted Ngai

Technologist / Researcher / Architect

Biography.01

Ted Ngai is a technologist, researcher, and architect whose career spans three distinct but interconnected disciplines. Trained as an architect at Harvard's Graduate School of Design and SCI-Arc, he has spent two decades working at the intersection of design, data, and emerging technology.

As Executive Director of Advanced Technologies at Pratt Institute, Ted is responsible for the institution's technology direction. He leads end-to-end AI architecture — from on-prem GPU data center infrastructure and data pipeline automation to large language model deployment, data analysis, retrieval-augmented generation systems, and AI agent implementation. His work bridges the administrative and academic sides of the institution, translating complex technical capabilities into practical solutions that enhance decision-making, streamline operations, and unlock new possibilities for research and education.

Prior to his work in technology leadership, Ted was a researcher at the Center for Architecture, Science, and Ecology, a joint initiative between Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute and Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. His specialization was data-driven building envelope design. His research produced the Active Modular Phytoremediation System — an award-winning air-cleaning wall technology deployed at the New York Police Department's Police Signal Administration Center II building. This work earned the Good Green Design Award and Architect Magazine's R&D Award, and culminated in a US patent for energy-exchange building envelope technology.

As an architect, Ted's achievements include an AIA Henry Adams Medal, 2nd prize in the international Butakovka Housing Development competition, and a design-build practice (UNI) recognized by the Boston Society of Architects and the New York Architectural League. His work has been published in Dwell, Wallpaper*, Architectural Review, and A+U.
